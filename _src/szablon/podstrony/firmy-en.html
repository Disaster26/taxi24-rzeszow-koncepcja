Taxi Rzeszów 24H/Business and hotel taxi

Business rides in Rzeszów and beyond

We drive employees, business partners and hotel guests around Rzeszów, to Rzeszów-Jasionka Airport (RZE), to the railway station and on longer trips - Kraków, Katowice, Lublin and Warsaw airports, Przemyśl and the Medyka border crossing. Available 24/7, so a late arrival or an early departure is no problem.

Airport pick-ups for guests

For airport pick-ups we track the flight for delays, waiting time is free, the car is parked opposite the terminal exit and, on request, the driver waits inside with a name sign. Drivers speak English, German, Polish and Ukrainian. More: Rzeszów Airport taxi.

Invoices and payment

Every ride can be invoiced (VAT invoice). Pay by card, cash, BLIK or bank transfer. City rides follow our price list; airport transfers and long routes are priced individually and confirmed before the ride. Our cars: Audi A7, Toyota Camry, BMW 5 Series, Toyota RAV4 and Ford Mondeo estate. We also offer courier rides, shopping on request and vehicle relocation.

Questions from companies

Do you issue VAT invoices for taxi rides?

Yes, we issue VAT invoices to companies. Give us your company details when booking.

Can you pick up our guest at Rzeszów Airport?

Yes. Send us the guest's name and flight number - we track the flight, waiting time is free and, on request, the driver waits in the terminal with a name sign.

Can a hotel book rides for its guests?

Yes, we work with hotels. Rides can be invoiced to the hotel, or the guest pays by card, cash or BLIK.
