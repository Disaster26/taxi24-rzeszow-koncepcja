From Rzeszów Airport straight to Przemyśl or Medyka

Rzeszów-Jasionka (RZE) is the nearest major airport to Przemyśl and the Medyka-Shehyni border crossing. Instead of travelling into Rzeszów and looking for a train or bus, you can go door to door:

  • Give us your flight number - we track your flight for delays.
  • Waiting time is free of charge.
  • The car is parked directly opposite the terminal exit; on request your driver waits inside the terminal with a name sign.
  • Your driver helps with luggage.

The same works the other way: we pick you up in Przemyśl or at the Medyka crossing and take you to your flight at Rzeszów Airport, also at night or early in the morning. More about airport transfers: Rzeszów Airport taxi.

Rzeszów - Przemyśl, both ways

Przemyśl is about 90 km from Rzeszów, mostly on the A4 motorway - usually around an hour's drive in normal traffic. We pick you up at your address, hotel or the railway station and drop you at the door in Przemyśl (home, hotel, Przemyśl Główny station). Booking for a group, with children or with lots of luggage? Let us know and we will send a larger car (Ford Mondeo estate or Toyota RAV4).

Taxi to and from the Medyka border crossing

Medyka is a little over 10 km past Przemyśl, about 100 km from Rzeszów. We drop you off at the Medyka border crossing on the Polish side and pick up travellers who have crossed from Ukraine. Because border waiting times vary, simply call or message us on WhatsApp once you are on the Polish side and the driver will come to the agreed spot. We speak English, Polish, German and Ukrainian.

How much is a taxi from Rzeszów Airport to Przemyśl or Medyka?

These routes are priced individually, depending on pick-up point, time and number of passengers. We confirm the price by text message or phone before the ride. For reference, our out-of-town meter rate is 8 PLN/km by day and 11 PLN/km at night, on Sundays and holidays, plus a 10 PLN starting fee - longer routes can be negotiated. Pay by card, cash or BLIK; companies get a VAT invoice. Full rates: taxi prices.
