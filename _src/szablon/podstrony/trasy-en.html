Arriving? Give us your flight number. We track your flight for delays and the driver waits at the terminal exit - on request with a name sign. Departing? We agree a pick-up time with enough margin for check-in. No transfers, no waiting for the last train.

Price

Long-distance rides are priced individually and confirmed by text or phone before the ride; longer routes can be negotiated. For reference, the out-of-town meter rate is 8 PLN/km by day and 11 PLN/km at night, on Sundays and holidays (taxi prices). Pay by card, cash or BLIK; VAT invoice for companies.

Cars and drivers

Audi A7, Toyota Camry, BMW 5 Series, Toyota RAV4, Ford Mondeo estate - tell us about extra luggage and we will send a larger car. Drivers speak English, German, Polish and Ukrainian.
