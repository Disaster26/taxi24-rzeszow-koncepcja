Questions about taxis and Jasionka transfers

Does Taxi Rzeszów 24H run around the clock?

Yes, we drive in Rzeszów and the surrounding area 24 hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.

Can I book a taxi to Rzeszów-Jasionka Airport?

Yes. We take you to Rzeszów-Jasionka Airport and pick you up from it, and we also drive to the airports in Kraków, Katowice, Lublin and Warsaw. Rides can be booked in advance.

Do you drive to Przemyśl and the Medyka border crossing?

Yes. Rides from Rzeszów to Przemyśl and to the Medyka border crossing with Ukraine are available on request, at any time, including at night. We give you the price when we confirm your booking.

What if my flight is delayed?

The driver tracks arrivals, so the airport pick-up happens at the right time even if your flight is late. Just add your flight number to the booking.

How much is a taxi to Jasionka Airport?

We give you the price when we confirm your booking by SMS or phone. Book using the form or call +48 536 805 851.

How do I book a taxi in Rzeszów?

By phone on +48 536 805 851, via WhatsApp or with the booking form on this page. We confirm every booking by SMS or phone.

Do you issue invoices to companies?

Yes, we provide business transport and issue invoices to corporate customers.

Which languages do you speak?

We take bookings in Polish, English, German and Ukrainian. WhatsApp is the easiest way to reach us.
